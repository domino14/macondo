**Timing.** We are behind, but there is a lot of game left. Unless the deficit
is 100 points or more, there is a good chance to come back, and a small deficit
while on turn is almost a positive.

Do not lean on the deficit. If the game is within 50 points and there are still
plenty of tiles in the bag, mention it once at most - it is not the reason this
play is best.
