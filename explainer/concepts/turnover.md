**Turnover.** Playing as many tiles as possible was once treated as a priority
in itself. It isn't any more - a good leave usually matters more.

It does matter here, though: we are in the second half of the game and there
are still tiles unseen that can swing it. Turning over more tiles gets us to
them sooner, and denies the opponent the same chance. You can often see the
effect in the data, where plays that use more tiles have a higher mean score
for us next turn - which is not normally the case.

Only raise turnover if the numbers actually show it. Otherwise leave it alone.
