**Bingos.** Playing all seven tiles is worth 50 points on top of the play's
score, and the player who bingoes first wins around 70% of the time. Going for
one is a common plan, though playing more defensively can be right when ahead -
and sometimes a bingo when already ahead is what ends the game.

A bingo chance of roughly 20% is what an ordinary turn looks like. Over 50%
means we are fishing and the fishing is working; 75% or more is exceptional and
is often reason enough for a play to be best on its own.

Our next bingo % can be low while our mean next score stays high. That usually
means we are keeping tiles that score well on this particular board, or that a
setup is in play.
