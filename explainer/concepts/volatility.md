**Volatility.** The standard deviation of the next scores - ours and the
opponent's - is a measure of how wild the position gets after a play. The
candidates here differ enough in it to be worth a sentence.

When you are ahead you want the standard deviation low: fewer swings means the
lead holds. When you are behind you want it high, because you need a swing to
happen.
