**Needed Draw.** In the follow-up table, the Needed Draw column is the tiles we
would have to draw from the bag, on top of the leave from the play we are
analyzing, before that follow-up is playable. `{SE}` means we need to draw both
the S and the E. A blank is written `?`.

An empty cell means the play needs no draw at all: we already hold everything
it takes.
