**The read changed the answer.** Without the inference this position has one
best play; with it, another. That is the most instructive thing an explanation
can carry, so lead with it and name both plays.

Explain the mechanism, not just the fact. The read makes some of the
opponent's replies likelier and others nearly impossible, and the recommended
play is the one that holds up against the racks they are actually likely to
have. Point at the specific tiles doing the work.

Keep the caveat attached: this is the right play *if the read is right*. The
other play is what to make when you don't trust it.
