**They found it.** The play being asked about is the one the simulation ranks
first. Open by saying so plainly, in a sentence, without ceremony.

Then teach the thing they can still learn: the head-to-head below is against
the runner-up, so explain what their play beat and why. Someone who picks the
right move for a vague reason will not pick it again in a position that looks
slightly different; naming the mechanism is what makes it repeatable.
