**The opponent's next turn.** Opponent numbers are less definitive than ours,
because we don't know their rack - the simulation drew representative racks for
them. Our own next plays are usually the more important half of the analysis.

What we can read is the aggregate: their bingo % and their mean and standard
deviation. Their bingo chance is high enough here to be worth mentioning, and
holding it down is a real reason to prefer one play over another.
