**Equity versus win%.** Equity is the combination of a play's score and the
value of its leave. It is usually not worth mentioning - except when a play
with higher equity comes out lower on win%, which is what has happened here.

That is worth explaining: the recommended play is giving up some points-plus-
leave in exchange for a better chance of actually winning the game. Say what it
is buying with them.
