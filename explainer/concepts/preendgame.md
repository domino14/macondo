**Late game.** There are 100 tiles in the game, so the bag starts with 86 once
both players have drawn. Once the bag is empty the endgame begins.

Be careful with the word "pre-endgame". It properly means the point where the
bag has fewer than 7 tiles and exchanging is no longer permitted. With 7 to 14
tiles left you can say the endgame is approaching, but do not call it a
pre-endgame. With more than 14 tiles in the bag it is never a pre-endgame.

This late, what you keep and what you leave the opponent matters more than raw
scoring, because there are only a few turns left to make up a difference.
