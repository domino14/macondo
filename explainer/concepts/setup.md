**Setups.** A setup is a play that creates a high-scoring chance for your next
turn, if you draw the right tiles or already hold them.

The "Follow-up chances" section has settled which follow-ups are genuine
setups. A play marked SETUP is one the engine confirmed does not exist on the
board until we make the recommended play - that is the strongest reason a play
can be best, and it should lead your explanation.

The other entries are plays the board already allowed, which we get to make
because of the tiles this play leaves us. Those are worth pointing out too -
they are often the real reason a leave is good - but call them what they are,
chances the leave buys, not setups.

Name the follow-up in full, with its coordinate, and say what has to be drawn
for it. If a follow-up needs the opponent to play something first, say so -
otherwise the reader will try the play and find it isn't legal yet.
